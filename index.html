Sources: 615 Warcraft Logs reports for the guild, Raider.IO guild rankings, and the TWIC Raiding Planner kill history.

Method: Counts cover Normal, Heroic and Mythic raid nights that were logged, Sep 21, 2016 to Oct 9, 2026. A few nights were never uploaded, so the true totals run a little higher. Hours run from the first pull of each night to the last. Duplicate logs of the same night are merged. People are counted with the guild's character-to-person crosswalk, compiled by Botokai: 326 characters mapped to 131 people. 256 other characters, about 6% of raid spots, are unidentified alts or one-night drop-ins and are not counted as people.
